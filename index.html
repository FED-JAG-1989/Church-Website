PRACTICAL SUPPORT. A WAY FORWARD.

Struggling with fines?
You may have options.

Heavenly Hope Church Incorporated is an approved sponsor of Victoria’s Work and Development Permit (WDP) scheme. We’re here to help you explore a non-financial way to deal with eligible fine debt.

Enquire about WDP ↗ Read Fines Victoria’s guide ↗ (opens in a new tab)

HOW THE SCHEME WORKS

  1. Check whether you’re eligible

    The scheme supports people experiencing vulnerability or disadvantage. Eligibility and the types of fines that can be included need to be assessed.

  2. Explore approved activities

    If eligible, you can reduce fine debt by taking part in approved activities or treatment under an approved sponsor’s supervision. Contact us to discuss the activities we can offer.

  3. Take the next step with support

    Email our team to discuss your circumstances and the WDP process. Please don’t include fine notices, identity documents or sensitive personal information in your first email.

A WDP is not an automatic fine waiver. Participation is subject to eligibility, eligible fines and approval under the scheme. Fines Victoria’s guidance explains the current requirements and exclusions.
